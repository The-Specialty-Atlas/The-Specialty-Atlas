Method & sources.

Every number on this site has a source; every score has a formula. Here is all of it — including what the scores are not.

01 / How it was built

Methodology.

This guide was compiled in August 2026 from: (1) NRMP Match data (2024–25), (2) CaRMS R-1 data (2025), (3) national pay scales (AAMC, NHS, TV-Ärzte, PARO, SCFHS, DHA/HAAD, QCHP), (4) physician-compensation surveys (MGMA/Medscape ranges), and (5) the AI-in-medicine literature (BLS projections, FDA clearances, screening trials).

Salary and hours figures are planning ranges, not offers — they vary with hospital, city, sector and seniority. Competitiveness scores are synthesised from match-rate statistics and IMG acceptance patterns.

The specialty catalogue covers every core residency specialty recognised in the US (ACGME), Canada (RCPSC), the UK (GMC), Germany (BÄK), the Gulf (SCFHS/DHA/QCHP) and home-country accreditation bodies, plus every recognised fellowship/subspecialty — ~200 fields in total. A handful of ultra-niche fellowships (e.g., specific site-based surgical fellowships) are grouped under their parent's fellowship list rather than listed individually.

Fit scores (0–100) are computed from the rubric in the next section using your questionnaire answers. They are a decision aid, not a destiny — the Top 10 section explains the reasoning behind each pick.

02 / The formula

The fit rubric.

Fit = Σ (weight × normalised trait score) × 100, plus small corrections: extra training years cost points unless income is very high, and surgical-family fields get a +3 passion bonus reflecting stated preferences. Competitiveness converts US scores to a 0.45–0.90 multiplier curve. The weights below are the default atlas profile — change them to match your own values and the ranking is yours.

03 / Reading the numbers

The legends.

Competitiveness (0–10)

Competitiveness is scored 0–10, where 10 = brutally competitive (top-tier USMLE/board scores, publications, honours; high unmatched rate), 8–9 = very hard, 6–7 = competitive but achievable with a strong CV, 4–5 = moderate, ≤3 = accessible. US scores reflect US-graduate difficulty; as a non-US IMG, add ~2 points of real-world difficulty for US/CA residency, and subtract 0–1 point for Gulf/Germany/UK where home-country MDs are well accepted.

AI danger (0–10)

AI danger 0–10 = how exposed the specialty's core work is to automation/commoditisation (10 = severe). It is NOT the same as 'jobs will disappear' — see the AI section for the augmentation paradox (e.g., radiology jobs are growing ~9% through 2034 even as AI reads images). Procedural, relational and responsibility-bearing work is hardest to automate.

Hours

Hours are typical in-training and early-attending averages. US resident hours are capped at 80/week by ACGME; UK/Germany/Gulf hours are contract-based (48–56/week) but calls add more.

Currencies

All salaries are gross annual averages in USD unless noted, converted at 2025–26 rates (SAR 3.75/USD, AED 3.67/USD, QAR 3.64/USD, £1≈$1.30, €1≈$1.10, CHF 1≈$1.15, CA$1≈$0.74). Ranges reflect seniority, region and sector (public vs private). Treat as planning estimates, not offers.
